Site reliability has become a strategic priority for modern organizations. While adopting cloud-native architectures opens new possibilities, it also introduces substantial operational complexity.

SRE teams constantly balance delivery speed with platform stability. Error budgets offer a practical framework for these trade-offs: when a service exhausts its budget, feature deployments pause until reliability improves.

Effective observability is equally vital. Teams must correlate metrics, logs, and traces to cut down mean time to detection and mean time to resolution. Without unified visibility, troubleshooting drags on and incidents become significantly more costly.

Organizations that invest in core SRE practices, such as automation, blameless postmortems, and service-level objectives, are far better positioned to run resilient systems at scale.
