# Understanding Microservices

Moving to microservices is a major transition in distributed computing that reshapes how engineering teams build and deploy software.

A successful setup generally relies on three core areas:
- Modularity: Breaking monoliths into independently deployable services.
- Resiliency: Preventing cascading failures with automated circuit breakers and retry policies.
- Scalability: Dynamically adjusting compute resources to handle changing demand.

API gateways and event-driven backbones further enable communication across different systems and domain boundaries. Together, these patterns give teams a practical foundation for scaling modern applications.
