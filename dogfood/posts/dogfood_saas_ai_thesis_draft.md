If AI becomes capable enough that companies believe they can replace software developers with it, the same capability also lowers the barrier for competitors and customers to replace or recreate SaaS products. Anything your business can suddenly build with broadly available AI, another business can probably build with broadly available AI too.

The point is not that AI will kill every SaaS company. That is too simplistic. The real issue is that AI commoditizes software creation. If implementation gets dramatically cheaper, implementation alone becomes much less of a moat.

That pushes durable differentiation toward things such as:
- Proprietary data
- Distribution and customer relationships
- Trust and workflow integration
- Operational execution and network effects
- Deep domain knowledge and switching costs
- Brand and genuinely unique technology

A company celebrating that AI makes its developers replaceable should probably ask whether AI also makes part of the company's product replaceable.
